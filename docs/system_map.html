1

Biometrics & Routine

Mifflin-St Jeor Engine BMR / TDEE

Weight, Height, Age, Biological Profile & Activity Level. Target = TDEE ± Goal Deficit (Max 1200 floor).

Solo Cook Routine 1-Serving

Meal Frequency (2/3/4/5 slots or custom) + Weekday effort matrix (Low effort BF <12m, Moderate lunch 25m).

2

AI Grocery & Pantry

Multimodal OCR Scanner Vision AI

Scans physical grocery receipts, Blinkit/Zepto PDF bills, or fridge snapshot images into structured items.

Pantry-to-Plate Engine Stock Match

Categorizes pantry items (Pulses, Dairy, Veggies, Spices) & computes Recipe Match % (100% In-Stock vs Missing).

3

Safety Shield & Data

Authoritative Data ICMR / USDA

USDA FoodData Central + ICMR-NIN (IFCT 2017). LLM never writes or alters calorie/macro facts (Rule 1).

Hard Safety Gate Rule 2 (Pre-Filter)

Allergen flags (tree nuts, dairy), vegetarian, gluten-free, medical constraints filtered BEFORE any scoring.

4

ML Scoring & Validation

6-Factor Hybrid ML SHAP Scoring

Score = 0.30×Macro + 0.20×Goal + 0.20×Pref + 0.15×Feedback + 0.10×Diversity + 0.05×Prep.

100% Macro Validator Dynamic Sync

Dynamically proportions calories, carbs (50%), protein (20%), and fats (30%) across all active meals.

5

Hinglish Kitchen

Hinglish Step-by-Step 1-Person Prep

Clear instructions with flame levels, spoon/cup measurements, and single-pan dishwashing hacks.

Adaptive Feedback Loop Ridge Regression

Post-meal ratings update preference_weights vector for future personalized recommendations.
