Paneer Bhurji: Quick Hinglish Edition

1. Ingredients Check

200g Low Fat Paneer check
1 Diced Onion & Tomato check

2. Solo-Cook Hinglish Steps

  1. Tadka Time: Kadai ya pan mein 1 tsp oil/ghee garam karo. Usme thoda jeera aur hari mirch daalo jab tak crackle na ho.
  2. Sauté: Barik kata hua pyaz daal ke 2-3 minute chalao jab tak translucent na ho jaye. Phir haldi, namak aur lal mirch add karo.
  3. Crush & Toss: Paneer ko grinder mein mat peeso! Haath se rough crumble karke pan mein daalo and 3 minute medium flame par mix karo.
